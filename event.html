Join authors KJ Ramsey, Laura Fanucci, and Elizabeth Berget for an intimate literary evening at Art House North in St. Paul, Minnesota.

Three writers. Three books. One evening exploring what sustains us when suffering strips everything else away — and how joy, stubborn and surprising, endures anyway.

Come for the stories. Stay for the conversation. Leave with something to carry you through.
